1. open loop: policies are applied to prevent congestion before it happens. the congestion control is handled either by the source or the destination
2. closed loop: are used to treat or alleviate congestion after it happens. several techniques are used by different protocols
	1. backpressure
	2. choke packet technique
	3. implicit signaling 
	4. explicit signaling 
		1. forward signaling
		2. backward signaling
